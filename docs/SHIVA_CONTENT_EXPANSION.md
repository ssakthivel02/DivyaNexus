# Shiva Legacy Content Expansion

This legacy-site enhancement is additive. It must not remove the existing broad Vedic knowledge scope.

## Priority Shiva pillar
- Shiva / சிவபெருமான் overview and names/forms
- Parvati / Uma / Shakti relationship and traditions
- Nataraja and Chidambaram
- Dakshinamurthy
- Ardhanarishvara
- Shiva Lingam traditions
- Pancha Bhoota Sthalams
- Jyotirlinga overview with source distinctions
- Major Shaiva temples and pilgrimage context
- Shaiva literature, hymns and philosophy with source labels

## Broader knowledge retained
- Vedas and Upanishads
- Bhagavad Gita
- Vishnu / Perumal and Lakshmi
- Devi traditions
- Ganesha
- Murugan / Skanda
- Temples and pilgrimage
- Siddhars, saints and acharyas
- Philosophy and life guidance

## Editorial controls
- Separate scriptural, historical, traditional, scholarly and oral claims.
- Do not present devotional beliefs as independently verified historical facts.
- Preserve citations/provenance where available.
- Avoid guaranteed health, wealth, marriage, employment or other outcome claims.
