# DivyaNexus Editorial Depth Wave 5

Date: 28 July 2026

## Objective

Expand weak or placeholder portal sections without hiding uncertainty, inventing current facts, or presenting editorial starter material as primary scripture or a complete encyclopedia.

## Delivered scope

- 36 bilingual editorial records across six collections.
- Rishi and lineage orientation records.
- Festival pathways with regional and calendar boundaries.
- Context-sensitive glossary records.
- Bounded life-guidance prompts.
- Pressure-free learning pathways.
- Family-safe kids learning pathways.
- Search coverage for Tamil, English, and common transliteration aliases.
- Visible editorial status badges.
- A public `/collection-status` transparency page.
- Replacement of `In preparation` directory cards with usable, bounded pathways.
- Editorial registry validation in pull-request, deployment, and nightly workflows.
- Browser regression coverage for search, directories, mobile layout, and status labels.
- A production-safe contract for the owner-selected artwork.

## Record labels

### Ready for guided learning

The bounded editorial pathway is suitable for portal use. This label does not certify every underlying edition, historical claim, regional practice, or traditional interpretation.

### Editorial orientation

The record helps a learner identify context, terminology, attribution, or variation before deeper source study.

### Needs source review

The topic is useful as a visible study lead, but record-level citations, current facts, specialist editions, or local verification are still required before expansion.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

The new records do not provide:

- current festival dates or temple timings;
- ritual or fasting instructions;
- health, legal, financial, or crisis advice;
- guaranteed devotional or supernatural benefits;
- unsupported historical dates or merged biographies;
- claims that one regional practice is universal;
- unrestricted child chat or child-data collection.
