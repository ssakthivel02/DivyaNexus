# DivyaNexus Content Review Matrix

| Collection | Records | Portal use | Additional review required before deeper claims |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Rishis & lineages | 6 | Orientation and attribution method | Record-level textual witnesses, historical scholarship, lineage-specific sources |
| Festivals | 6 | Regional and cultural context | Current dates, calendars, local practice, travel and temple arrangements |
| Glossary | 8 | Bilingual context-sensitive vocabulary | Text-specific translation, school-specific doctrine, specialist terminology |
| Life guidance | 6 | Bounded educational reflection | Medical, legal, financial, counselling, safeguarding, or crisis matters remain outside scope |
| Learning | 6 | Pressure-free guided pathways | Reviewed editions, expert curriculum review, recording or assessment evidence |
| Kids | 6 | Shared family learning | Age-band testing, educator review, safeguarding review, caregiver guidance |

## Review dimensions

Every future record should be evaluated across these dimensions:

1. **Record identity** — stable unique ID, route, collection, title, and Tamil title.
2. **Source type** — primary text, translation, commentary, historical scholarship, living practice, oral tradition, or editorial synthesis.
3. **Attribution** — who or what tradition makes the claim.
4. **Date sensitivity** — whether the information can become outdated.
5. **Regional sensitivity** — whether practice differs by location, community, language, or lineage.
6. **Risk boundary** — whether health, safety, legal, financial, travel, child, or crisis implications exist.
7. **Translation boundary** — whether one English or Tamil equivalent hides meaningful differences.
8. **User action** — whether the portal merely teaches context or could influence a consequential decision.
9. **Review status** — ready, editorial orientation, or needs source review.
10. **Evidence retention** — exact source, edition, page, URL, review date, and reviewer notes when available.

## Publication rule

A record may be useful before it is complete, but its incompleteness must remain visible. A missing source is not repaired by confident wording.
