## Priority corrections

The shared system must be rebuilt before route-by-route work: a cinematic visual-layer system, a compact responsive header and footer, a non-obstructive audio pattern, richer image-first homepage chapters, stronger display hierarchy, and motion that is pointer-enhanced on desktop but touch- and reduced-motion-safe elsewhere. The next version should reserve text panels for reading, not use them as the principal visual material of every route.
