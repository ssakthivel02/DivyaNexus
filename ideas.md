## Chosen Approach: Divine Observatory Cinema

### Design Movement

DivyaNexus will become a **cinematic sacred-knowledge observatory**. It combines gallery-grade visual storytelling, modern AI-product clarity, and a respectful Vedic atmosphere without copying a devotional website, a museum catalog, or the reference product. The experience shifts from a dark editorial interface toward a living universe of layered scenes, art direction, and purposeful interaction.

### Core Principles

1. **Every major section is a scene.** A journey should move between cosmic horizons, manuscript light, temple scale, learning trails, and intimate reflection rather than repeating dark content cards.
2. **Spectacle must guide understanding.** Visual richness is never a substitute for accurate labels, source boundaries, readable navigation, or action clarity.
3. **Depth is earned through layers.** Image, light, geometry, typography, and interaction create depth; generic gradients, oversized rounded panels, and decorative noise do not.
4. **Mobile is a distinct choreography.** The compact experience uses intentional story cards, carousels, safe-area spacing, and touch feedback instead of merely stacking desktop layouts.

### Color Philosophy

**Cosmic Indigo** carries the night-sky foundation, paired with **Divya Saffron (`#F0A43A`)** as the unmistakable signal for curiosity, action, and living knowledge. Dawn peach and lamp gold introduce warmth at section thresholds, while manuscript ivory creates reading clarity and quiet temple stone anchors deep content surfaces. Violet appears only as low-saturation atmospheric depth; the system must not read as generic purple SaaS.

### Layout Paradigm

The site follows an **immersive visual procession**. The hero is a cinematic stage, followed by alternating large-image chapters, offset split-scenes, scrollable story rails, constellation maps, and compact knowledge surfaces. Each route begins with a contextual visual threshold and then transitions to reading or interaction regions. Instead of repeated grids, the layout switches rhythm between wide landscapes, asymmetric columns, orbital clusters, and touch-first galleries.

### Signature Elements

1. **Lightway Arcs:** Fine glowing paths, orbital trajectories, and moving points connect questions, sources, and learning paths across the site.
2. **Illuminated Manuscript Frames:** Visual panels carry a warm gold inner edge, soft grain, and source pin rather than generic card borders.
3. **Lotus Threshold Geometry:** Layered lotus, arch, and yantra-inspired structures introduce scenes, organize galleries, and anchor motion without depicting sacred text as decoration.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like **revealing a layer of a living archive**. Desktop pointer movement creates a subtle light response, shallow art-layer parallax, and constrained card tilt. On touch, the same hierarchy is available through clear tap, swipe, and bottom-sheet patterns. The flagship Ask Divya experience uses a luminous knowledge core, visibly-labelled answer layers, and conversation routes rather than a generic message bubble. Every nonessential motion honors reduced-motion settings.

### Animation

Motion follows the pace of breath and light: a 12–18 second atmospheric drift for background glows, a 180–260ms tactile response for controls, 280–420ms editorial reveals for scenes, and a maximum 3° pointer tilt for artwork. Cursor effects run only on fine-pointer devices using `requestAnimationFrame`; touch uses press and horizontal-drag feedback. Animated particles, geometry, and parallax become static or nearly static under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Nothing essential depends on motion.

### Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** remains the cinematic display face for high-impact English statements and source titles. **Manrope** anchors interactive and explanatory text with stronger contrast and shorter mobile measures. **Noto Serif Tamil** is used for Tamil-led statements and transliteration companions, with deliberate line-height and size rather than squeezed interface labels. Display typography shifts from editorial restraint toward confident museum-scale headlines, while source data remains compact and clear.

## Style Decisions

- Every major homepage chapter needs a strong original visual, visual texture, or interactive scene; a text-only dark band is not acceptable.
- Hero and feature art use cinematic landscape or portrait crops with explicit text-safe areas, alternate crop positions, lazy loading beyond the hero, and non-repeated assets.
- Shared surfaces are based on illuminated-manuscript frames, lightway arcs, and lotus threshold geometry—not generic rounded purple panels.
- The mini player is a compact launcher by default, adds safe-area-aware document clearance, and only expands into a bottom sheet after intent.
- Mobile navigation, galleries, and Ask Divya are touch-first compositions; hover-only affordances are never essential.
- Legal routes remain quiet and readable but retain a small visual threshold and a compact accordion footer.
- Every route contains a non-card scene primitive: cinematic landscape threshold, illuminated manuscript chamber, constellation path map, lotus/yantra threshold, or temple-architecture corridor.
- Scripture and study surfaces use manuscript ivory, a warm inner edge, source pin, and archival texture rather than only dark panels.
- Ask Divya remains a luminous knowledge-core ritual with labelled source, generated reflection, and next-step layers; it is never framed as a generic chatbot.
