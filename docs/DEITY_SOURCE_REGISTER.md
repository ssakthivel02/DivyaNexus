# DivyaNexus Deity Source Register

Date reviewed: 28 July 2026

## Registered orientation sources

### The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Recognizing the Gods

- URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/gods/hd_gods.htm
- Use: recurring iconographic attributes, vehicles, relationships, and broad visual identification.
- Limit: museum orientation is not a substitute for primary texts or living theological traditions.

### The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Ganesha, Tamil Nadu, Chola period

- URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/37397
- Use: elephant-headed form and Shiva–Parvati family context.
- Limit: one object record does not establish every regional Ganesha form.

### The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Saraswati, Goddess of Learning and Music

- URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/74840
- Use: vina, book, rosary, lotus, and learning-and-music iconography.
- Limit: this modern artwork record does not cover the full textual history of Sarasvati.

### The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Kumara Riding a Peacock

- URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/38333
- Use: Kumara/Skanda/Karttikeya naming, family relationship, and peacock vehicle.
- Limit: Nepalese object context is not identical to every Tamil Murugan tradition.

### The British Museum — Śiva collection biography

- URL: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG16395
- Use: iconic and aniconic forms, Nataraja, meditation, Nandi, and family orientation.
- Limit: concise collection biography; specialist textual review remains necessary.

### The British Museum — Kārttikeya (Skanda) collection biography

- URL: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG11335
- Use: Karttikeya, Skanda, Murugan, Subrahmanya, Shanmukha, and Kumara naming pathways.
- Limit: labels broad cross-regional traditions and does not replace Tamil literary study.

### The British Museum — Śiva Natarāja object record

- URL: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1987-0314-1
- Use: dancing Shiva, anandatandava, and Apasmara object context.
- Limit: object interpretation must remain distinct from temple worship and dance pedagogy.

### Smarthistory — Hindu deities

- URL: https://smarthistory.org/hindu-deities/
- Use: scholarly introduction to the diversity of Shaiva, Vaishnava, and Goddess traditions.
- Limit: introductory overview rather than a record-level textual authority.

### Smarthistory — Shiva as Lord of the Dance (Nataraja)

- URL: https://smarthistory.org/shiva-as-lord-of-the-dance-nataraja/
- Use: art-historical orientation to Nataraja and Chola-period visual culture.
- Limit: should be paired with temple, textual, and tradition-specific sources in future waves.

### IIT Kanpur — Gita Supersite

- URL: https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/
- Use: primary-text and commentary pathway for Krishna in the Bhagavad Gita.
- Limit: deity overview pages do not reproduce a verse unless a specific edition and translation record is reviewed.
