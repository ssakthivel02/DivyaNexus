# DivyaNexus Readability and Audio Wave 8

## Production defects addressed

1. The owner-selected portal artwork existed in production but was only presented in a later homepage showcase, so it was not visible as the primary hero at initial load.
2. Several dark surfaces and muted text colours were too subdued at normal browser zoom.
3. Tamil text required stronger font fallback, line height, weight and manuscript contrast.
4. The audio page accurately reported that no reviewed recording existed, but offered no working listening aid.

## Implemented response

- The owner-selected WebP is promoted into the first active homepage hero scene.
- Hero imagery receives a modest brightness, saturation and contrast adjustment.
- The hero veil is lighter while retaining text contrast.
- Dark-theme muted text and panel boundaries are brighter.
- Tamil uses Noto Sans Tamil first, with larger minimum text, stronger weight and increased line height.
- Scripture manuscript Tamil receives dark foreground colours on a brighter paper surface.
- The audio page now provides user-initiated browser speech in Tamil and English.
- Every audio option includes a visible transcript.
- Users can choose Tamil or English and adjust reading speed.
- Playback can be stopped immediately and never autoplays.

## Audio truth boundary

Browser speech is an accessibility and reading aid generated by a voice installed in the user's browser or operating system. It is not a reviewed human recording, Vedic recitation, traditional chant, or authoritative pronunciation source. Voice availability and Tamil pronunciation quality vary by device.

Reviewed human audio remains a separate future content type requiring:

- named speaker or recording provenance;
- visible transcript;
- pronunciation review;
- source and edition context;
- rights and reuse status;
- non-autoplay playback.

## Automated validation

Static validation requires:

- owner artwork hero promotion;
- Tamil font and minimum-size contracts;
- visible transcript controls;
- Tamil and English speech languages;
- speech-synthesis implementation;
- speed control;
- explicit non-reviewed-recording language;
- no autoplay.

Playwright validates:

- primary hero artwork visibility and brightness treatment;
- Tamil scripture font size, line height, family and contrast;
- working speech controls using a deterministic browser mock;
- Tamil and English transcripts;
- user-initiated playback status;
- responsive overflow at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels.
